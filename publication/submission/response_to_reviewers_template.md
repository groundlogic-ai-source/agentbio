## Standing response points (pre-drafted; adapt as needed)

**"The audit study failed — why publish?"** The failure is the finding: the
internal regression suite (12/12 traps) implied a strong audit layer, while
the frozen external study measured 0.533 defect recall. Publishing the
instrument design *and* its null result is the methodological contribution;
suppressing it would be exactly the bias the study was built to detect.

**"Increase the sample size."** Agreed as future work; the current n is fixed
by the pre-registered case list and one-scored-run rule. CIs are reported so
readers can weigh precision; any expansion is a new pre-registered study.

**"Fix the detected defects and re-run."** Fixes (pool staleness, N3
precision) will be evaluated in new pre-registered studies. Re-scoring this
frozen claim set after fixes would convert a confirmatory instrument into an
exploratory one; we decline to do that and say so.

**"Clinical utility?"** None is claimed. The system is a research
prioritization and evidence-audit tool; the abstract, methods, and
limitations state this explicitly.
