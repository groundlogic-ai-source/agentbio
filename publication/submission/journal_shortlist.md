# Journal shortlist (ranked) — sequential submission only

Ranked by scope fit for a *methods + validation* contribution that reports a
negative audit result honestly. Preprint-policy pages checked 2026-08-10
(re-verify at submission time — policies change). Submit to **one journal at
a time**; only proceed to the next after a rejection or a withdrawal you
initiated. The bioRxiv preprint stays up regardless (all five venues below
accept preprinted work).

## 1. Journal of Cheminformatics (BMC / SpringerOpen) — first choice
- **Why:** core audience is exactly this paper's (cheminformatics pipelines,
  evidence sources, validation methodology); publishes negative/validation
  results; fully open access; BMC explicitly permits preprints.
- Article type: Research (methodology). Check APC waiver policy if needed
  (BMC has waivers for unfunded/independent authors — apply before
  submission, not after acceptance).
- Policy page: jcheminf.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines

## 2. PLOS Computational Biology
- **Why:** strong fit for benchmarking methodology and reproducibility;
  explicit preprint support incl. bioRxiv direct transfer (verified
  2026-08-10: journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/s/preprints).
- Risk: higher selectivity; the honest-FAIL framing must be sold as the
  methodological contribution (audit-layer external validation design), not
  as a system paper.

## 3. PLOS ONE
- **Why:** soundness-over-impact review explicitly welcomes negative and
  null results; preprints welcomed (journals.plos.org/plosone/s/preprints).
- Good fallback if J Cheminform declines on scope.

## 4. PeerJ (Life & Environment / Bioinformatics)
- **Why:** soundness-based review, preprint-friendly culture (PeerJ
  Preprints heritage; author instructions verified 2026-08-10:
  peerj.com/about/author-preprint-instructions), reasonable APC, independent-
  researcher friendly.

## 5. F1000Research (last resort / parallel-publication model)
- **Why:** publishes methods and null results with transparent post-
  publication peer review; gates on soundness. Note its model *is*
  preprint-plus-open-review, so posting on bioRxiv first is optional here;
  useful if traditional venues stall.

## Explicitly not shortlisted
- *Bioinformatics* (OUP): allows preprints but strongly favors novel
  algorithms over validation/audit studies; high desk-reject risk for a
  negative audit result.
- *Nature Communications / Genome Medicine etc.:* impact profile mismatch;
  a single-pipeline validation with a failed audit is out of scope.
- *Drug Discovery Today*: reviews/commentary-led; a methods validation
  article is a weak fit.

## Process discipline
1. Submit to #1 only. Tailor the cover letter (template provided).
2. On rejection: incorporate addressable comments, then submit to the next
   venue; keep a log (`review_log.md`) of submissions, dates, and decisions.
3. Any new analysis motivated by reviewer comments must be labeled **post
   hoc** in the revision (see response-to-reviewers template) — frozen
   studies are never re-scored to satisfy a reviewer.
4. Update the preprint with the accepted version when the journal allows.
