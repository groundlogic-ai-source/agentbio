# Timothy syndrome literature-limitation postmortem

_Post-benchmark production finding. This document does not edit, rerun, rescore,
or relabel any frozen benchmark, audit, or publication artifact._

## What the case established

The Timothy syndrome / nisoldipine case remains an auditable pipeline result,
but it is not an externally prioritizable flagship. The target-level evidence
supports only that nisoldipine has pharmacology against CACNA1C and that a
coarse “gain of calcium-channel function → channel inhibition” direction can
look plausible. It does not establish correction of the TS1 p.G406R/exon 8A
gating defect, benefit in a disease model, adequate human exposure, or clinical
efficacy.

## The missed limitation

Timothy syndrome is a mutation-specific channel-gating/inactivation disorder,
not simply excess total calcium influx. The 2026 international language and
management-guidelines consensus statement reports the contemporary management
landscape and is the authoritative source that motivated this post-benchmark
production correction:

- *Timothy syndrome and CACNA1C-Related Disorder: first international language
  and management guidelines consensus statement*, European Journal of Human
  Genetics (2026), DOI-linked article:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41431-026-02178-8

The production defect was architectural: the mechanism-direction stage asked
whether action on the named target opposed the broad disease defect. It did not
separately ask whether consensus, clinical, or mechanism-specific literature
already limited the proposed drug class for the exact disease subtype and
intended use.

## What remains valid

- The persisted target pharmacology and its source provenance.
- The original evidence score as a measurement under the policy that produced it.
- The conclusion that the hypothesis can be inspected as a failure-analysis case.

## What is withdrawn

- Any implication that nisoldipine is uniquely preferred over other
  dihydropyridines.
- Any implication that target-level directional compatibility establishes
  mutation rescue or a viable treatment strategy.
- External prioritization of calcium-channel blockers for the proposed TS1
  cardiac-electrophysiology use when an applicable high-confidence limitation
  is confirmed.

## Production correction

The new literature-limitation lane is retrieval-first and citation-bound. It
requires exact disease, intended-use, and drug/class applicability; verifies
that the quoted text is present in the retrieved PubMed record; and applies a
block only for a guideline/systematic review or at least two independent
explicit records. Conflicts and source failures remain visible unknowns. A
confirmed limitation preserves the evidence score, removes a coarse
directional bonus, and closes external prioritization and paid structure
validation.
