## Interpretation guardrail

A `qualified_directional` row is evidence-bearing and directionally described, not proof that it is a valid disease hypothesis. A `none_or_identity_only` or `unqualified_or_unresolved` row is not support for a credible competing hypothesis. Human review of the underlying target and disease mechanism is still required.
